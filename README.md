# Byke Rider

**“Everybody gets a ride home.”**

Byke Rider, usually called **Byker**, is a crimson-haired horseman, driver, and transport specialist working with the Epsilon Mercenary Group. He has operated everything from tractors to salvage submarines, but his favorite ride will always be a black horse.

Friendly, rebellious, and a little too eager to accept a driving challenge, Byke brings muddy boots, roadside kindness, and questionable shortcuts to a world of mercenary contracts and dangerous artifacts.

## Setting and use

Byke is written to fit the supplied Dent, Hex, Huntress, and Shimmer examples: capable but flawed people pursuing money, loyalty, knowledge, or personal obsessions in a gritty world where unusual artifacts exist. His specialty is getting a team into trouble and, preferably, back out again.

The character sheet is the main reference. Roleplay hooks are suggestions for the group to adopt, not established facts about other players' characters. The current mission is left open so Byke can join an existing campaign.
