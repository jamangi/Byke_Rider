## Original card (verbatim)

```text
EPSILON GROUP — "The Natural Order Preserved"

Epsilon are mercenary crusaders, not soldiers for hire. They systematically destroy reality-warping artifacts, anomalous spaces, and beings that violate natural law. Founded on Human Primacy—the belief that humanity's greatness comes from struggle, not supernatural cheats.

Doctrine: Anomalies are a cancer. They do not negotiate or study—they neutralize. Contain and collapse warped spaces. Shatter artifacts. Dispose of threats in sealed vaults.

The Prejudice: Epsilon won't execute civilians, but their contempt runs cold. Anomalous users are "Vectors," born anomalies are "Aberrants," creators are "Architects." All face segregation, professional disdain, and refusal of aid—even when it costs lives. They cloak bigotry in pity: "We do this for your own good—to purge the taint."

Structure: Cells deploy globally with military precision. Their emblem—stylized Greek E in dark green, black, and crimson—represents: Black (void after excision), Green (natural world preserved), Crimson (blood spilled willingly).

The Paradox: Their most effective anti-anomalous weapons are themselves anomalous. They justify it as "fighting fire with fire."

Recruits are veterans, disillusioned scientists, and anomaly survivors. They weep for those they contain while believing, absolutely, that they are righteous.

"The world was not meant to bend. We are the hands that hold it straight."
— Epsilon Command Directive 001
```
