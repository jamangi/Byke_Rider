# CHARACTER SHEET: BYKE RIDER

> “Everybody gets a ride home.”

## I. BASIC INFORMATION

**Callsign/Code Name:** Byke Rider / Byker

**Real Name:** Silas Ryder

**Gender:** Male

**Age:** 33

**Nationality:** American, Louisiana

**Physical Description:** 5'11", 180 lbs, lean and sturdy. Naturally black hair dyed crimson, hazel eyes, and a crooked grin. Wears a battered charcoal riding jacket, work trousers, boots, and a crimson neckerchief. Friendly and rebellious, with grease-stained hands and scarred knuckles.

## II. BACKGROUND

**Former Occupation/Specialty:** Stable hand / Driver and equipment operator / Transport and recovery

**Agency/Affiliation:** Epsilon Mercenary Group

**Brief Bio:** Raised around a struggling Louisiana stable, Byke learned horses first, then chased work operating tractors, cranes, semi-trailer trucks, and small salvage submarines under supervision. Bicycles, motorcycles, and amateur racecars satisfied his restless streak. Epsilon recruited him after he recovered their stranded transport with a tractor and returned for an injured contractor.

**Primary Motivation for taking this mission:** Keep Last Stop stable open, buy its pasture, and bring the crew home.

## V. GEAR

**Primary Weapon:** Lever-action carbine

**Secondary Weapon/Tool:** Heavy adjustable wrench

**Essential Equipment (3 items max):**

1. Mechanic's tool roll
2. Handheld radio with spare battery
3. Small first-aid pouch

## VI. VEHICLE

**Vesper, “Ves”:** A sure-footed black gelding, his favorite ride. Byke calls him “the finest vehicle ever built” and spoils him with apple pieces. Uses a battered diesel pickup and horse trailer for longer journeys; keeps Ves away from active gunfire.

## VII. FLAW/WEAKNESS

**Overconfidence:** Challenges to his driving ability make him overpromise and attempt unsafe routes or unfamiliar controls. Stubborn with authority, he risks pay and safety rather than abandon a teammate or animal—a loyalty enemies can exploit.
